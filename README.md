## Crafting Recipes

### Flour

1 Wheat

### Sugar Block

```text
SSS
SSS
SSS
```

`S` = Sugar

### Ground Beef

Use a stonecutter:

1 Cooked Beef → 3 Ground Beef

### Dough

3 Flour + 1 Water Bucket

### Raw Tortilla

```text
DD
```

`D` = Dough

### Tortilla

Cook Raw Tortillas in a furnace.

### Beef Taco

```text
BBB
 T 
```

`B` = Ground Beef
`T` = Tortilla

### Crunchwrap Supreme

```text
 T 
BBB
 T 
```

`B` = Ground Beef
`T` = Tortilla

### Baja Blast Syrup

* 1 Cyan Dye
* 2 Honey Bottles
* 6 Sugar Blocks

### Baja Blast Potion

Brew in a Brewing Stand:

* Water Bottles
* Baja Blast Syrup
